Brakes, wheels and drivetrain

Brake rotors

The metal discs on the wheel hubs that the brake pads grip.

Reviewed October 23, 2026.

Also called: disc rotors, discs

What it does

Larger rotors brake better and cope with heat, which is why many e-bikes use them. Many rotors have their minimum thickness stamped on them.

Signs of a problem

  • Pulsing when braking
  • Rubbing sound every wheel turn

Safe things to check

  • Look for bends or deep grooves.
  • Don't touch a rotor right after a long descent; it gets very hot.

Looking after it

  • Keep oil and lube off rotors.
