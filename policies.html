Shipping

We are not currently selling and shipping bikes directly. Bikes marked "Buy direct" are purchased from the brand, on the brand's own terms. The section below describes how our shipping will work once our dealer accounts are approved, and how freight delivery works generally.

Bikes will ship free to addresses in the lower 48 states. We do not currently ship to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, or internationally.

E-bikes ship by freight or oversized parcel carrier because of their weight and their lithium battery. Most orders leave the warehouse within one to three business days and arrive within five to ten business days of dispatch. Freight carriers call ahead to schedule delivery — give the carrier a number you actually answer at checkout, since missed delivery appointments are the single most common cause of delay.

Inspect before you sign

Look the carton over before signing the delivery receipt. If you see crushed corners, punctures, or a shifted load, note the damage on the receipt before signing and photograph it. A signed clean receipt makes a freight claim much harder to win. Contact us the same day and we will handle the claim.

Bikes built on our bench are local pickup only. Once a bike is assembled it can't be economically reshipped, and the battery would require hazmat handling a second time. Assembly orders are collected in person.

Returns

Applies to bikes purchased from us. For anything bought "Buy direct" from a brand, that brand's return policy governs — check it before you order.

You may return an unridden bike in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery.

  • Return freight is paid by the customer. On an e-bike this typically runs $150–$400 depending on distance and carrier.
  • A 10% restocking fee applies to returned bikes.
  • The bike must be in original, unridden condition with all packaging, hardware, charger and documentation.
  • Bikes that have been ridden, modified, assembled by a third party, or damaged are not returnable, though they may still be covered under warranty.
  • Assembly and service labor is not refundable once the work has been performed.

We would rather be honest about this than surprise you later: e-bikes are heavy, freight is expensive, and a "free returns" promise on a 70 lb item is usually paid for somewhere else. Ask us questions before you order and we will help you avoid needing a return at all.

To start a return, email us with your order number and we will issue instructions and a freight quote before anything ships back.

Warranty

Warranty coverage on every bike sold here is provided by the manufacturer of that bike, not by EbikeBuy. Coverage typically runs one to two years on the frame, motor, battery and controller, and terms vary by brand and by model. The specific warranty for your bike is stated on its product page and in the documentation that ships with it.

What we add: if you bought the bike from us, we will file and follow up on the warranty claim on your behalf at no charge. We will gather the photos, serial numbers and diagnostics the manufacturer asks for and chase the response so you don't have to sit in a support queue.

What we can't do is overrule a manufacturer's decision, extend their coverage period, or cover wear items. Tires, tubes, brake pads, chains, cables, grips and spokes wear out with use and are not warranty items on any brand.

Damage from crash, misuse, water immersion, unauthorized modification, or use of a non-approved charger will void most manufacturer warranties. This is worth reading closely before you modify anything electrical.

Assembly & service

Assembly and service is performed at Bikes Plus in Jensen Beach, Florida, and is offered on any e-bike from any brand, whether or not you bought it here. The service covers the full pre-delivery checklist published on our home page: uncrating and damage inspection, torquing every fastener to the manufacturer's specification, truing and tensioning wheels, centering and bedding in brakes, indexing the drivetrain, setting tire pressure to rider weight, a full charge cycle with battery log, display configuration, a five-mile road test, and fit adjustment at pickup.

You can drop the bike off, or have it shipped directly to the shop still in its carton — tell us the tracking number and we will take delivery and inspect it for you. Turnaround is typically three to five business days. We will contact you when the bike is finished and the checklist is signed off. Bikes not collected within 30 days of completion may incur a storage fee.

Service is a workmanship service, not a warranty. If something we adjusted comes out of adjustment within 30 days, bring it back and we will correct it at no charge. Component failure remains a manufacturer warranty matter.

Riding safely and legally

Florida law treats Class 1, 2 and 3 electric bicycles as bicycles. Class 3 bikes have riding restrictions in some municipalities and on some paths. Local ordinances vary, helmet requirements vary by age, and it is the rider's responsibility to know and follow the rules where they ride. An e-bike is a vehicle: wear a helmet, ride within your ability, and don't modify the controller to exceed its class limit.
