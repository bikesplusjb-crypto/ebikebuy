How to read the badges

Three wheels

If balance is the reason you stopped riding, a trike is the answer — and it's the one thing you really shouldn't assemble from a box yourself.

Why we push assembly hardest on these

A trike has a rear differential, a wide axle, and baskets that all have to be squared to the frame. Get it wrong and the trike pulls to one side or the rear wheels fight each other in a turn — which is exactly the instability you bought three wheels to avoid. It's also 80-plus pounds in a carton that one person can't safely flip. Have it shipped to us and skip all of that.
