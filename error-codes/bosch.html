Bosch 3-digit error codes

Bosch drive systems that show 3-digit codes on Intuvia, Kiox, Purion, Nyon or SmartphoneHub displays. Purion displays may show only the last two digits (for example 03 for 503). The newer Bosch smart system shows 6-digit codes instead; look those up on Bosch's site for now.
